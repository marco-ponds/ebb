  How to use this spec
  Build Ebb, an offline-first, installable PWA for timing contractions, exactly as drawn in the
  screenshots below. Everything is stored on the device, with no accounts, no network calls
  after install and no analytics. The app records and describes; it never diagnoses or says
  whether someone is in labour.

  For Claude Code:

       Treat the screenshots and the Design tokens section as the visual source of truth. The
       canvas source files ( .dc.html ) are reference HTML: copy their spacing, radii and copy,
       not their runtime.
       Treat every acceptance criterion (AC) as a test to write. ACs are numbered by screen,
       e.g. LT-04.
       When this spec and the screenshots disagree, the spec wins for behaviour and the
       screenshots win for layout.
       Store raw timestamps only. Every number on screen is derived (Data model section).
       Never add red, alarms, countdowns or medical labels. The Copy rules section is a hard
       constraint, not a style preference.

  Users. The labouring person taps start/stop one-handed, in pain, often in the dark. The
  birth partner usually holds the phone, reads numbers out to a midwife and edits entries.
  The phone passes between them, and a session can run for 12+ hours with the screen on.

  In scope for v1: live tracking, intensity, events, sessions, history, session detail with charts,
  comparison, user thresholds, share text, PDF/CSV export, JSON backup and restore,
  settings.

  Out of scope for v1: accounts, cloud or partner sync, push notifications, clinical advice,
  pregnancy tracking, kick counting, hospital bag lists.

  Design tokens
  Put every value below into one tokens file (CSS custom properties) and use nothing
  outside it. The aim is calm: a dusk-plum night background, pastel accents that each mean
  one thing, and no pure white, red or flashing.


  Typeface: Nunito
       Family: Nunito (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License). Chosen for its rounded
       terminals, which read as soft, and its clear figures.
       Self-host it. Bundle the woff2 files for weights 600, 700, 800 and 900 with the app and
       precache them in the service worker. Don't load from Google Fonts at runtime,
       because the app must work offline from first load.
       Fallback stack: 'Nunito', ui-rounded, 'SF Pro Rounded', system-ui, sans-
       serif .
       Every number uses font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so timers don't jitter as
       digits change.
       Use font-display: block for at most 300 ms, then swap, so the timer never flashes
       in the fallback font on reopen.

       Breathing glow: a soft peach disc behind the contraction button that grows from 0.7
       × to 1 × of 420 px (1.4 × the button; clipped by the screen, and by the left column
       in landscape) at 40 → 90 % opacity as you breathe in, then shrinks and fades as you
       breathe out. Breathe in and Breathe out are set separately in Settings (3–8 s each,
       default 4 s + 4 s, an 8 s cycle). Ease-in-out, centred, starting on the inhale; a
       timing change applies from the next contraction. The glow sits under the other
       content and never takes taps. It is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion and by
       the Settings toggle.
       Transitions: 200–250 ms ease-out colour crossfade between resting and contracting.
       No bounces, shakes or flashes anywhere.
       Icons: 2–2.4 px stroke, rounded caps and joins (Lucide is a good match). No emoji.


  Data model and derived metrics
  Store raw timestamps in IndexedDB and compute everything else. Write each tap to disk
  before updating the UI, so a crash or a lock never loses a tap. All times are epoch
  milliseconds (UTC); display uses the device's local time zone.

  Derived metrics
  For contractions c₁…cₙ in a session, ordered by startAt , counting only finished, non-
  deleted ones:

     \text{duration}_i = \text{end}_i - \text{start}_i \qquad \text{interval}_i =
     \text{start}_i - \text{start}_{i-1} \qquad \text{rest}_i = \text{start}_i -
     \text{end}_{i-1}


       Time apart (UI label) = interval. Rest is shown only in session detail.
       Rolling averages over the last 30 and 60 min: the mean of intervals whose start falls in
       the window. Show — until there are at least 2 intervals.
       Per 10 min: the count of contraction starts in [now − 10 min, now); charts use fixed 10-
       min buckets from the session start.
       Regularity = the coefficient of variation of intervals in the last 60 min, needing at least
       4 intervals. CV < 0.20 → Regular; 0.20–0.40 → Fairly regular; > 0.40 → Irregular. Always
       show the range too ("every 4–6 min", the 10th–90th percentile, rounded to whole
       minutes).
       Progression: the least-squares slope of interval, duration and intensity against start
       time over the last 60 min (at least 5 points). Interval slope < −0.5 min/h → "getting
       closer together"; > +0.5 → "getting further apart"; else "steady". Duration slope beyond
       ±5 s/h → "getting longer" or "getting shorter". Intensity slope beyond ±0.5/h → "getting
       stronger" or "easing off".
       Gap: an interval more than 2.5 × the median of the previous 5, and at least 15 min. Draw
       it as a dashed empty box on the timeline. Exclude it from regularity and progression.
       Formatting: under 60 s → 58s ; under 60 min → 4m 10s ; otherwise 1 h 15 m . Timers
       on buttons use m:ss (and h:mm:ss past 1 h).


  App lifecycle: open, close, resume
  There is no in-app loading screen. On a cold start the OS shows the manifest splash (Dusk
  background, lavender "ebb" mark). The app shell then paints from the service-worker
  cache in under 500 ms and lands directly on the right screen. The rules below decide
  which screen that is.


  Where the app lands on open
   1. No onboardedAt → Welcome (first launch, below).

   2. A contraction with endAt = null → Live tracker, contracting state, with elapsed time

       Install hint: shown only when not running standalone. On iOS: "Tap Share, then Add to
       Home Screen." On Android/Chromium: an Install button using beforeinstallprompt .
       Primary button: Get started. It calls navigator.storage.persist() , sets
       onboardedAt and goes to Start. Thresholds aren't asked for here; they live in Settings.


  While a session is active
       Wake lock: request navigator.wakeLock.request('screen') when a session starts
       or the app becomes visible with an active session (if Keep screen awake is on). Re-
       acquire it on every visibilitychange to visible. Release it when the session ends.
       Wake lock pill: "Screen stays on" in mint while held. If the API is missing or the request
       fails, it reads "Screen may sleep" in --text-faint , with no alert.
       Timers: tick with requestAnimationFrame , throttled to 1 Hz for display. Always
       compute from stored timestamps, never from an incrementing counter.
       Rolling stats recompute on every tap and every 15 s.


  Closing, locking and backgrounding
       Nothing needs saving on close, because every tap is committed before the UI changes.
       Never rely on beforeunload or pagehide to persist data.
       If the phone locks or the app is killed mid-contraction, endAt stays null. On reopen the
       contracting state resumes with the true elapsed time (LT-11).
       Stale contraction: on reopen, if an in-progress contraction is older than 5 min, the
       button still shows the elapsed time. Above it appears a quiet card: "Still going? This
       one's been running 23 min." with two actions, Ended earlier… (opens a time picker for
       the end) and Keep timing.
       Long pause: on reopen with a resting session and no contraction for 60+ min, show
       "Contractions paused? Last one 1 h 12 m ago." with Keep session going and End
       session…. Never end a session automatically.


  Updates (service worker)
       Precache the app shell, fonts and icons at install. Runtime: cache-first for same-origin
       assets. There are no network requests for data, ever.
       A new version installs in the background but never activates or reloads during an
       active session. When no session is active, it activates on the next cold start. Settings
       shows "Update ready — restart the app to use it" until then.

  User journeys
  These seven journeys are the end-to-end tests. Each step names the screen and what
  must happen.


  J1. First contraction of the night (labouring person, alone, one hand)
   1. Opens Ebb from the home screen and lands on Start.
   2. Taps Start timing. A session is created and Live opens in the resting state with "No
      contractions yet" and the button reading Tap when it starts.
   3. A contraction begins: one tap anywhere on the button. It turns peach, a soft haptic
       plays (if on), and the timer counts up from 0:00.
   4. It eases: one tap. The button returns to lavender and the strength picker slides in
      above it.
   5. Ignores the picker; it closes by itself after 30 s. Intensity stays empty and can be added
       later.


  J2. Partner takes over and reads numbers to the midwife
   1. Partner picks up the phone. Live already shows the last time apart and length in large
       type.
   2. The midwife asks how often. Partner reads "every 4m 20s over the last 30 minutes,
       lasting about 55 seconds".
   3. Taps Share. A sheet shows the summary paragraph with Copy and Share… (Web Share
      API). Partner reads it aloud or texts it.
   4. Taps Event → Called midwife. A mint marker appears on the mini timeline.


  J3. A mis-tap
   1. The person taps start by accident. The button turns peach.
   2. Taps Started by mistake. The contraction is discarded and the button goes back to
      resting, with no confirmation.
   3. Or, after stopping a contraction they didn't mean to: taps Undo within the resting
       state, which restores the contraction to in-progress with its original start time.

  J4. Threshold met
   1. Earlier, in Settings, the partner switched on 3 in 10 min, as the midwife advised.
   2. After a contraction ends and the rule is satisfied, the butter banner appears: "Matches
      the threshold you set — 3 in 10 min."
   3. The banner stays while the rule holds and can be dismissed with ×. It reappears only if
       the rule stops holding and then holds again.


  J5. Waters break, with a gentle prompt
   1. Taps Event → Waters broke, then picks a colour (Clear, Pink, Green or brown, Not
       sure). Time defaults to now and can be changed.
   2. The event saves. Selecting Green or brown, Bleeding or Reduced baby movement
      shows one calm line below the confirmation: "If you haven't already, contact your
       midwife or maternity unit." It has no colour change, no icon and no countdown.


  J6. Fixing the record later
   1. Opens the session from the Live header (taps "Tonight") or from History, and lands on
       Session detail.
   2. Taps the pencil on a row and edits start, end, strength and note in a bottom sheet with
      time pickers. Saving recomputes all charts.
   3. Taps Add a missed contraction, enters start and length, and it slots into order.
   4. Renames the session to "Saturday morning" by tapping the title.


  J7. Ending, comparing and backing up
   1. On Live, presses and holds "Press and hold to end session" for 1.5 s. A ring fills under
       the finger; letting go early cancels.
   2. A sheet asks "End this session?" with a summary and End session / Keep going. Ending
      sets endedAt and returns to Start.
   3. Days later, opens History → selects Tonight and Tuesday → Compare.
   4. In Settings → Back up everything, a ebb-backup-2026-10-25.json file is downloaded
      or shared. The Start screen's backup button then shows "Back up · today".

  Screens and acceptance criteria
  Each AC is one testable statement. Routes are hash-based so the app works from any
  static host: #/ , #/live , #/history , #/session/:id , #/compare?ids=a,b , #/settings .


  Live tracker (LT): screens 1–3
  The layout from top to bottom: the header (session name linking to detail, elapsed time,
  wake-lock pill), two stat cards, the averages card, the pattern line, the hero button (flex:
  1), the mini timeline (linking to detail), the action row, and the end-session control.

       LT-01 Tapping anywhere on the hero button toggles resting ↔ contracting. A tap
       commits to IndexedDB before the UI changes, and the UI responds within 100 ms.
       LT-02 Resting: lavender, labelled "Tap when it starts", with a big timer for time since the
       last start (or "No contractions yet" with no timer).
       LT-03 Contracting: a 300 px peach circle labelled "Contraction" above and "Tap when it
       eases" below, with an elapsed m:ss timer. The header and stat cards drop to 55 %
       opacity.
       LT-04 Taps within 600 ms of the previous toggle are ignored (debounce) to absorb
       double taps.
       LT-05 The stat cards show the last interval ("Time apart") and last duration ("Lasting"),
       or — when unknown. Values never wrap; they shrink to 32 px if needed.
       LT-06 The averages card shows the last 30 and 60 min as "every {interval} · {duration}
       long", or "Not enough yet".
       LT-07 The pattern line shows regularity · progression in plain words, with a mint dot for
       Regular, butter for Fairly regular and faint for Irregular. It is hidden when there's too
       little data.
       LT-08 When a contraction ends, the strength card appears with five options (Mild…
       Intense) shown as growing dots plus words, a numeric 1–10 row, or both (per Settings).
       Choosing one saves and closes it. Skip closes it. With no choice it closes after 30 s,
       with a thin progress bar. It closes immediately if a new contraction starts.
       LT-09 The mini timeline shows the last 60 min: bar x = start, width = duration (min 5 px),
       height = intensity (unrated = middle height, hollow outline). Event markers are circles.
       A dotted line marks now.
       LT-10 Undo reverts the last action (start, stop, rating, event, edit) in one tap, with no
       confirmation. A toast reads "Undone" for 3 s. Undo depth is 1. During a contraction the
       left action is labelled Started by mistake.

  Thresholds and copy rules
  The app describes what happened and points back to the user's own plan. It never
  interprets the pattern medically.


  Threshold evaluation
       Evaluate all enabled thresholds after each contraction ends, after an edit, and every 60
       s.
       N in M: at least N contraction starts in the last M minutes.

  Accessibility, PWA and performance

  Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)
       All text is at least 4.5:1 against its background. Pastel fills always carry --ink text.
       Use real elements: <button> , <a> , <input> , <label> , <table> with <th scope> .
       Toggles are role="switch" with aria-checked ; the strength picker is a radiogroup .
       The hero button's accessible name includes state and time, e.g. "Start contraction. 3
       minutes 42 seconds since the last one started". An aria-live="polite" region
       announces "Contraction started" and "Contraction ended, lasted 58 seconds".

       optional glow, which uses CSS only and pauses when hidden.
